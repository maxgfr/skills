# The grill

Ask every decision whose prerequisites are settled in one round, most consequential first, then wait. Number them for good and recommend, so a round is answered in one line:

**Q-001 — <title>**: <the decision and its options>
→ <your recommendation, and why>

Each answer goes into `## Locked constraints`; "your call" locks your recommendation, marked delegated. Stop when nothing is open.
